Wheat / grain hex (hex_grano):
  "Catan Tiles grain/Dessert and harbor pieces" by Jaimz079
  https://www.printables.com/model/926286-catan-tiles-graindessert-and-harbor-pieces

  Wheat field = grain albedo; Mill / MillRoof / Blades = mill textures;
  Hay = straw bake on the second mound; Side = wood.
  Mill albedo maps (CC0 Poly Haven): wood_floor, roof_09; sails = local canvas bake
  in art/props/molino_{madera,tejas,lona}.jpg; hay = art/props/fardo_paja.jpg.
